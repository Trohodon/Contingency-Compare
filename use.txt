AC Mitigation Project Meeting Notes
September 22, 2026

PURPOSE
Coordination meeting for AC mitigation studies on several pipelines in the project area. Notes below focus primarily on items relevant to Transmission.

TRANSMISSION / POWER DATA
- AC modeling will consider transmission facilities within a 2-mile radius of the pipeline corridor.
- Approximately 19–20+ transmission lines/circuits have been identified in the study area.
- The consultant has already prepared a Power Data Request identifying the specific lines and data needed.
- Some facilities in the study area appear to be owned by Georgia Power. If Dominion cannot provide a contact, the consultant will pursue the needed data.
- Consultant asked whether power lines have been added/changed since the existing mitigation was installed (~2011), as system changes could contribute to increased AC induction.
- For the new turbine/facility project, current understanding is that no new transmission circuits are being added. Some existing poles/structures may be relocated. This should be verified.
- Any new or proposed transmission lines should be identified in the Power Data Request response.
- Actual power-system data is preferred. Conservative assumed data could result in unnecessary additional mitigation.
- Power data may take 6–8 weeks to obtain and is one of the major schedule drivers.

KEY PROJECT INFORMATION
- Existing 6-inch pipeline has recorded AC readings as high as approximately 140 V.
- Consultant believes the newer, unmitigated 6-inch line may be contributing to the high readings on the older line because the pipelines are tied together.
- Existing 6-inch mitigation dates to approximately 2011 and has limited records/monitoring. Consultant will price field verification of the existing mitigation separately.
- Existing 6-inch line is Zap-Lok pipe; consultant will evaluate whether this affects the AC model.
- Stations are included in the AC mitigation scope, including proposed facilities where plans are available.
- Project will likely be engineered as four subprojects/drawing packages:
  1. 20-inch pipeline
  2. 8-inch coal-tar pipeline
  3. Existing mitigated 6-inch pipeline
  4. Newer 6-inch pipeline
- Dominion requested one combined proposal/bid.

SCHEDULE / NEXT STEPS
- 90 days was considered aggressive; approximately 5 months was considered achievable.
- Major schedule dependencies are power data and soil-resistivity testing.
- Immediate information needed for the consultant's proposal is primarily existing/proposed station information rather than power data.
